# Privacy Policy for Mosul Shop (سياسة الخصوصية لتطبيق الموصل شوب)

**Last Updated: February 13, 2026**

Welcome to **Mosul Shop (الموصل شوب)**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your **Phone Number** and **Name**.
- **Delivery Information**: We collect your **Location** and **Address** to facilitate the delivery of your orders.
- **Order History**: We keep a record of your purchases to help you manage your orders and for customer support.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To process and deliver your orders.
- To manage your account and provide customer support.
- To send you important notifications regarding your orders.
- To improve our services and user experience.

## 3. Data Sharing
- We share your name, phone number, and delivery address with **Merchants** and **Drivers** only for the purpose of fulfilling your orders.
- We do **not** sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

## 4. Data Security
We use industry-standard security measures (including secure servers and encryption) to protect your data from unauthorized access.

## 5. Your Rights
- **Access and Correction**: You can view and update your profile information within the app.
- **Data Deletion**: You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the "Contact Us" section in the app.
