# Account Deletion Policy (سياسة حذف الحساب)

At **Mosul Shop (الموصل شوب)**, we believe you should have full control over your data. If you wish to stop using our services and delete your information, you can do so easily.

## How to Delete Your Account
1. Open the **Mosul Shop** app.
2. Go to the **Account (حسابي)** screen.
3. Tap on the **Delete Account (حذف الحساب)** button.
4. Confirm your choice.

## What Happens When You Delete Your Account?
- **Immediate Deletion**: Your profile information (Name, Phone Number) will be immediately removed from our active databases.
- **Order History**: Past order records may be retained for accounting and legal purposes but will be anonymized.
- **Irreversible**: Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. You will need to register again if you wish to use the app in the future.

If you encounter any issues deleting your account, please contact our support team.

يمكنك حذف حسابك وكافة بياناتك الشخصية بسهولة:

1. افتح تطبيق **الموصل شوب**.
2. انتقل إلى شاشة **حسابي**.
3. اضغط على زر **حذف الحساب**.

**ماذا يحدث عند الحذف؟**
سيتم مسح اسمك ورقم هاتفك فوراً من قواعد بياناتنا. هذا الإجراء نهائي ولا يمكن التراجع عنه.
